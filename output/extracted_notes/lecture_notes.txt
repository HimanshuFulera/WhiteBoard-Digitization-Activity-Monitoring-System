MONETARY POLICY
*   The branch of economic policy which manipulates the Money Supply in a society to regulate the aggregate economy.
*   Handled by the Central Bank. In the U.S., it's called the Federal Reserve System (The Fed).

MONEY SUPPLY
*   The amount of money in an economy available for use by households, businesses, and governments for purchases.
*   Forms of Money (Physical):
    *   Currency
    *   Deposits in Banks

KEYNESIAN ECONOMIC THEORY
*   MARGINAL PROPENSITY TO CONSUME (MPC)
    *   The additional amount of money consumers spend when they receive one more unit of income.
    *   Example: If $100 in income leads to $93 in consumption, the MPC is 0.93.

TYPES OF RESOURCES
*   Fixed Resources: Limit a firm's productive capacity.
*   Variable Resources

UTILITY MAXIMIZATION
*   The process by which a consumer chooses the best mix of quantities of products to consume, given limited resources, to achieve the highest possible level of satisfaction.
*   Tools:
    *   Utility Schedule
    *   Marginal Utility Per Price
    *   Marginal Utility: The additional satisfaction (utility) gained from consuming one more unit of a good or service.
